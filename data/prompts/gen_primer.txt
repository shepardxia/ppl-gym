`Gen` and `Random` are already imported (`using Gen`).

Define the model with `@gen function m(args...) ... end`. Inside it a random choice is `{:name} ~ dist(args)` with a unique address, indexed inside loops (`{:s => i} ~ ...`); distributions include `bernoulli`, `categorical`, `normal`, `beta`, `gamma`, `dirichlet`, `uniform`, `uniform_discrete`, `poisson`. A raw draw outside a model is `Gen.random(dist, args)`.

For exact inference over discrete latents: give the observations as `choicemap((:addr, value), ...)` and an enumeration grid over the unobserved latents as `choice_vol_grid((:latent, [values]), ...)`, call `enumerative_inference(model, (args...,), obs, grid)`, and reduce the result to a distribution over the model's return value with `enum_dist(...)`. Sample-based inference uses `Gen.mh` or `Gen.importance_sampling`. Add a soft/log-weight term with a `{:name} ~ factor(logweight)` choice, observed at `0.0` in the choicemap.
