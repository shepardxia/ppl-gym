`pyro`, `pyro.distributions as dist`, `pyro.infer`, and `torch` are already imported.

A random choice is `pyro.sample("name", dist.<Name>(args))`, with a unique site name (index it inside loops, e.g. `f"x{i}"`). Distributions live in `dist` (`Bernoulli`, `Categorical`, `Normal`, `Beta`, `Gamma`, `Dirichlet`, `Binomial`, `Poisson`, `Uniform`, ...). Condition with `obs=` on a sample site or `pyro.factor("name", log_weight)`. Inference algorithms are in `pyro.infer` (`Importance`, `MCMC` with `NUTS`/`HMC`, `SVI`).
