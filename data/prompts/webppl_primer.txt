Sample with the lowercase primitives (`flip`, `uniform`, `gaussian`, `beta`, `dirichlet`, `randomInteger`, `uniformDraw`, `categorical`). The capitalized names (`Bernoulli`, `Gaussian`, `Beta`, `Categorical`, `Dirichlet`, `Binomial`, `Poisson`, ...) are distribution constructors, used with `sample(D)`, `observe(D, value)`, and as the return value of inference.

`Infer({method: ...}, modelFn)` runs `modelFn` and returns a distribution over its return value; methods include `'enumerate'`, `'rejection'`, `'MCMC'`, `'SMC'`, `'forward'`. Condition with `condition(bool)`, `observe(dist, value)`, or `factor(logWeight)`.
